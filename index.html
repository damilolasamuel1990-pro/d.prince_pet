About Us

We Love Pets As Much As You Do

D.Prince Pet NG is a pet shop dedicated to connecting loving families with healthy, well-raised pets. We started with a simple goal: to make quality pets and pet products easy to find for every Nigerian pet lover.

Every pet we sell is dewormed, vaccinated and checked by a licensed veterinarian before it goes home with you. We also give free after-sale advice on feeding, training and care.
